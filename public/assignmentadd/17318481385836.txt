Key Features to Implement:
User Authentication & Profiles:

Roles: Admins, teachers, students, parents.
Sign Up & Login: Email, phone, or third-party integrations (Google, Facebook).
Profile Management: Separate profiles with dashboards tailored to each user role.
Course Management:

Course Creation: Teachers should be able to create, edit, and delete courses.
Course Categories: Organize courses by subject, difficulty, or other filters.
Content Delivery: Videos, PDFs, assignments, and quizzes.
Progress Tracking: Track student progress, completion, and grades.
Live Classes & Video Conferencing:

Integration with tools like Zoom, Google Meet, or built-in live streaming.
Recording and playback for students who miss classes.
Quizzes, Assignments, and Exams:

Automated grading for multiple-choice questions.
Submission and grading of assignments.
Timed exams with features like auto-submit after time expires.
Student & Teacher Communication:

Messaging System: Chat or email-like communication between teachers and students.
Discussion Forums for each course.
Announcements for important updates.
Attendance Tracking:

Automated attendance for live classes.
Manual attendance marking for offline or asynchronous activities.
Payment Gateway Integration:

Subscription Plans: Monthly, yearly, or per-course fees.
One-time Purchases: Buy individual courses or materials.
Support for multiple payment methods like credit card, PayPal, Stripe, etc.
Reporting & Analytics:

Student Performance: Progress reports, grades, and course completion rates.
Course Analytics: Teacher effectiveness, engagement, drop-off points.
Certificate Generation:

Automated certificates for course completion.
Customizable templates for different courses or milestones.
Multilingual Support:

Language options for users to choose their preferred language.
Localization of content for a global audience.
Mobile-Friendly Design:

Responsive design for access on phones and tablets.
Native mobile apps if necessary for enhanced experience.
Admin Dashboard:

Manage Users: Add, remove, or edit user information.
Manage Courses: Approve or disapprove courses, monitor activity.
View Financial Reports: Subscription revenues, payment histories.
Business Logic to Consider:
Role-Based Access Control (RBAC):

Admins, teachers, and students should have different levels of access to functionality.
Course Lifecycle Management:

From course creation to publishing, enrollment, and eventually archiving after a set period.
Payment & Refund Logic:

Subscription handling, refunds for dissatisfied customers, trial periods, and discount codes.
Grading & Assessment:

Logic for calculating final grades from quizzes, assignments, and exams.
Pass/fail criteria based on weighted scores.
Content Availability:

Unlock content gradually (e.g., new lessons available weekly) or based on performance.
Student Progress Tracking:

Logic to track engagement and provide recommendations based on performance.
Suggested Database Tables:
Users:

id, name, email, role (admin, teacher, student, etc.), password, profile_info, created_at.
Courses:

id, teacher_id, title, description, category, price, start_date, end_date, is_active.
Course_Content:

id, course_id, content_type (video, PDF, etc.), content_url, content_description, order_number.
Enrollments:

id, user_id, course_id, enrollment_date, status (active, completed, withdrawn).
Quizzes/Exams:

id, course_id, title, total_marks, time_limit.
Questions:

id, quiz_id, question_text, options (JSON for MCQs), correct_answer.
Submissions:

id, user_id, quiz_id, submitted_answers, marks_obtained, submitted_at.
Payments:

id, user_id, course_id, payment_method, amount, payment_date, status.
Certificates:

id, user_id, course_id, issue_date, certificate_url.
Messages:

id, sender_id, receiver_id, message_content, timestamp, status (read/unread).
Attendance:

id, user_id, course_id, session_id, attendance_date, status (present/absent).
Reports/Analytics:

id, user_id, course_id, progress, score, last_accessed, feedback.
